### 道光庚子增修凡例 (1840)

* 舊譜分十卷首世系次世表次恩綸次冢墓次文傳次小傳次附記此次世衍丁繁卷帙日富因分為一十八卷次第一遵前例惟將文傳內內行傳誌另彙一卷為內傳又易附記為附譜其中簡帙繁多者分上中下卷世表行第字號科名官職封典生卒葬所妻室子女及有傳銘行署并行誼載入志乘者其行款胥循舊譜
* 舊譜世系倣蘇氏法累代相繼長則銜接本宗次則順列於後萬派一源瞭如指掌惟自始遷至今世歷十八而舊圖以三大分祖起行每行十三世字已甚密不能再增況以後生齒日繁奕葉遞衍自應稍變其體因酌定十世一行行末標明子幾人某某其繼與別房者不敘以歸簡省十一世上標明某人第幾子係立嗣者則稱嗣子遞相銜接仍分三分各敘支派秩然
* 舊譜世表倣歐陽體五世一行其五世另起處標明某人子不用復見以就簡省惟吾族自春鸝公始遷第三世有兄弟四人而華觀傑三公後皆不傳至第五世始分三大分今在毘陵者皆此三分子孫也支庶日繁幾於莫可辨皙故酌於世表中第五世三大分祖另起複見以下各分分錄仍循舊例庶宗派益覺分明
* 齒為達尊同於爵德故六十以上稱壽並詳其歲數亦古人引年之義也殤者不載其有年已成童及本支無後有待議嗣者仍行載入又舊譜有名旋即夭折者亦存之以備查考
* 舊譜中名諱有與廟諱御名同字不問久遠一律謹避用同音字代之仍註明於下
* 舊譜內所有墳圖此次均為附刊以垂永久現在各房子孫有願繪圖增入者均令各自捐貲刊刻其塋地坐落都圖村鎮字號畝分兆域山向墓田均照舊例詳敘彙入祠墓一門無考及漏未開送者闕
* 舊譜於祝子贅婿概不列入世次第於表系內註明惟其中有無宗可歸歷久仍繫吾姓者自應量為變通彙集譜後名曰附譜俾合族咸悉其來源所自以廣收族之仁即以杜冒宗之漸至表系內仍註明乞養子以符舊例亦不至於混淆
* 吾族宜興始祖為宋迪功郎泰然公而宜興譜與吾譜不符已詳舊例現有春鸝公弟懋溪公盛子孫現居荊溪渭瀆其譜亦與吾譜脗合世遠年湮無從確考自當各守傳聞異辭之義以示率由
* 吾族宗譜創自順治十年距始遷時已遠自乾隆辛亥輯修又越百數十年今又五十載矣分處鄉邑散失無考者不可勝數上次修譜時即有閔黃大樹基村等處赴局請修以推按舊譜不甚相符因作附記一卷詳其支系以俟詳覈今復有奔牛鎮後等支亦皆無宗派可接錄其世次歸入附譜以從闕疑之義其舊譜附記今未開送者亦綴數行以附於後
* 此次修譜仍照舊章各分捐下彙辦其有不敷係族中在外作宦遊幕者勉力捐輸另刻捐錄隨譜給發
* 此次舊譜編某人收領字樣號分給收領譜後列明第幾號某分印共一百部循以備續修查繳
* 壞者罰跪一炷香遺失不全者罰修石塔墓將殘譜收回公所仍令追尋所失本數務在必得查到日交分長收貯有不肖售與同姓者察知會同族眾於各分公祠中重處追出仍令罰修祠墓
* 家譜所以收渙散奠世次關係匪輕今約族眾於每歲石塔集祭時各報一年消長之數及諸事件三分各置簿一本本支分長或管年者詳為載記十年編成一草譜三十年一續修則皆耳目聞見所及為功尤易焉

### 白话文 简体字 带标点

【卷帙与体例】
卷数调整：旧谱分为十卷，顺序依次为世系、世表、恩纶（皇恩）、冢墓、文传、小传、附记。此次修谱因为家族繁衍，人丁兴旺，卷帙日益丰富，因此扩充为十八卷。
内容编排：编排次序基本遵循旧例。唯独将“文传”中的“内行传志”（记载女性德行的文章）单独汇成一卷，称为《内传》；又将“附记”改为《附谱》。其中篇幅繁多的内容，分为上、中、下卷。
世表格式：世表中的行第、字号、科名、官职、封典、生卒年月、葬地、妻室子女，以及有传记、墓志铭、行状且被载入地方志的人物，其排版格式完全遵循旧谱。

【世系图表法】
苏式画法改良：旧谱世系图模仿苏洵（苏氏）的法度，累代相继，长房衔接本宗，次房顺列于后，万派一源，清晰明了。但是，自从始迁祖到现在已经历十八代，旧图从三大分房祖先开始画起，每行画十三代，字迹已经非常密集，无法再增加。况且以后人口日益繁多，枝叶递衍，理应稍变体例。因此斟酌定为每行画十代，在行末标明有几个儿子、名字叫什么。过继给别房的就不叙述，以求简省。在第十一世之上，标明是某人的第几个儿子，如果是立嗣的则称为“嗣子”，递相衔接。仍然分为三大房，各叙支派，井然有序。

【世表文字法】
欧式画法改良：旧谱世表模仿欧阳修（欧阳体）的格式，五世一行，在五世另起的地方标明是谁的儿子，不再重复出现，以求简省。但我族自从春鹂公始迁，第三世有兄弟四人，其中华、观、杰三公的后代都没有传承下去，直到第五世才分出三大房。现在居住在毗陵（常州）的都是这三大房的子孙。支庶日益繁多，几乎难以分辨清楚。因此斟酌在世表中，第五世的三大分房祖先另行起头重复出现，以下各房分别记录，仍遵循旧例，这样宗派更加分明。

【年龄与殇逝】
尊长与夭折：年龄（齿）是与爵位、德行并列受尊敬的，所以六十岁以上称为“寿”，并详细记载岁数，这也是古人尊老的意义。未成年而死（殇）的不记载，但如果年纪已成童（约15岁以上）或者本支没有后代、有待商议立嗣的，仍然载入。另外，旧谱中有取了名字随即夭折的，也保留下来以备查考。

【避讳与坟墓】
避讳原则：旧谱中如果有名讳与庙讳（皇帝祖先名）、御名（皇帝本名）同字的，不管年代多久远，一律谨慎回避，用同音字代替，并在下面注明原字。
坟墓图谱：旧谱内所有的坟墓图，这次都作为附录刊印，以垂永久。现在各房子孙如果有愿意绘图增入的，都令各自捐资刊刻。墓地的坐落、都图、村镇、字号、亩分、兆域（墓地范围）、山向、墓田等，均照旧例详细叙述，汇入《祠墓》一门。无法考证及漏报未送的，暂缺。

【收养与异姓】
义子赘婿：旧谱对于义子（祝子/螟蛉子）、赘婿一概不列入世系表，只在表系内注明。但其中有些人无宗可归，年代久远仍系我族姓氏的，自应酌情变通，汇集在谱后，名为《附谱》，使全族都知道他们的来源，以推广收族的仁心，同时也杜绝冒认宗族的渐端。在世系表内仍注明是“乞养子”，以符合旧例，也不至于混淆。

【始祖与源流考证】
宜兴始祖：我族宜兴始祖是宋代的迪功郎泰然公，而宜兴谱与我们谱不符，已在旧例中详述。现有春鹂公的弟弟懋溪公（名盛）的子孙，现居住在荆溪渭渎，他们的谱也与我们的谱吻合。但因世远年湮，无法确考，自当各守“传闻异辞”的原则，以示遵循旧制。
待考支派：我族宗谱创修于顺治十年，距离始迁时已经很远了。自从乾隆辛亥年辑修后又过了一百几十年，现在又过了五十年。分散在各乡邑、散失无法考证的不可胜数。上次修谱时，就有闵黄、大树基村等处来局里请求修谱，因为推算按旧谱不太相符，所以作了一卷《附记》，详细记录他们的支系，以待详查核实。现在又有奔牛镇后等支派，也都无宗派可接，记录其世次归入《附谱》，以遵从“阙疑”（存疑）的意义。旧谱《附记》中现在未报送的，也缀写几行附在后面。

【经费与管理】
捐资刊刻：此次修谱仍照旧章程，由各分房捐款汇总办理。如有不够，由族中在外做官或当幕僚的人勉力捐输，另外刻印《捐录》随谱发给。
领谱编号：此次旧谱编有某人收领的字样，按号分给收领。谱后列明第几号、某分印，共印一百部，循环使用，以备续修时查缴。
惩罚措施：损坏谱书的罚跪一炷香，遗失不全的罚修石塔墓。将残谱收回公所，仍令追寻所失的本数，务必找回。查到之日交给分长收贮。有不肖子孙卖给同姓外人的，察觉后会同族众在各分公祠中重处，追回谱书，仍令罚修祠墓。
定期续修：家谱是用来收拢涣散的族人、奠定世次关系的，关系重大。现在约定族众在每年石塔集祭时，各报一年人口消长的数目及各种事件。三大分房各置簿一本，由本支分长或管年的人详细记载。十年编成一草谱，三十年进行一次续修。这样都是耳目闻见所及的事情，做起来尤其容易。

### The Writing Standards (1840)

Article 1:
The old genealogy was divided into ten volumes: first the lineage charts, then the lineage tables, imperial honors, tombs, biographies, short biographies, and appendices. This time, due to the proliferation of descendants and the increasing volume of records, it has been expanded to eighteen volumes. The order generally follows the old rules, except that the biographies of women's conduct have been compiled into a separate volume called "Inner Biographies," and the "Appendix" has been renamed "Supplementary Genealogy." Sections with extensive content are divided into upper, middle, and lower volumes. In the lineage tables, details such as birth order, style names, examination titles, official positions, honors, dates of birth and death, burial places, wives, and children, as well as those with biographies or local chronicle records, follow the formatting of the old genealogy.

Article 2:
The lineage chart of the old genealogy followed the Su Shi method, with generations connected in succession. The eldest branch connected to the main clan, and the younger branches were listed in order, making the single origin of all branches clear. However, from the first migrant ancestor to the present, eighteen generations have passed. The old charts started from the ancestors of the three major branches, with thirteen generations per row, and the text had become too dense to add more. Moreover, with the daily increase in population and the continuous branching out, a change in format was necessary. Therefore, it has been decided to list ten generations per row, marking the number of sons and their names at the end of each row. Those adopted into other branches are not listed to maintain simplicity. From the eleventh generation onwards, it is marked as "so-and-so's nth son," and if adopted, they are called "adopted son." They are connected in sequence, still divided into three branches, each with its own orderly lineage.

Article 3:
The lineage table of the old genealogy followed the Ouyang Xiu style, with five generations per row. At the start of a new five-generation block, the father's name was not repeated to save space. However, for our clan, starting from the migrant ancestor Lord Chunli, there were four brothers in the third generation, but the descendants of Lords Hua, Guan, and Jie were not transmitted. It was not until the fifth generation that the three major branches emerged, and all current members in Piling are descendants of these three. As the branches have become increasingly complex and difficult to distinguish, it has been decided to repeat the ancestors of the three major branches at the fifth generation in the lineage table, and then list each branch separately below, following the old rules, so that the branches are even clearer.

Article 4:
Age is a mark of respect equal to rank and virtue, so those over sixty are referred to as having "longevity," and their exact ages are detailed, following the ancient principle of honoring the elderly. Those who died young are not recorded, but those who reached adulthood (15 years old) or those whose branches have no heirs and are awaiting adoption are still included. Additionally, names from the old genealogy that were夭折 (died in infancy) are also retained for reference.

Article 5:
In the old genealogy, if a name shared a character with the imperial ancestral temple names or the emperor's personal name, it was strictly avoided regardless of how long ago it was, and replaced with a homophone, with the original character noted below.

Article 6:
All tomb diagrams in the old genealogy have been published as supplements this time to preserve them permanently. Descendants of each branch who wish to add diagrams must donate funds for engraving. Details such as the location, administrative division, village, plot number, area, boundaries, orientation, and tomb fields are described in detail according to old rules and compiled into the "Temples and Tombs" section. Information that is unverified or not submitted is left blank.

Article 7:
The old genealogy did not list adopted sons (Zhuzi) or sons-in-law living with the family (Zhuixu) in the lineage tables, only noting them in the lineage records. However, for those who have no clan to return to and have long borne our surname, a compromise has been made to collect them in the "Supplementary Genealogy" at the end of the book, so that the whole clan knows their origins, extending the benevolence of accepting relatives while preventing the gradual usurpation of the clan name. In the lineage tables, they are still marked as "adopted sons" to comply with old rules and avoid confusion.

Article 8:
Our clan's ancestor in Yixing was Lord Tairan, a Diligonglang of the Song Dynasty, but the Yixing genealogy does not match ours, as detailed in the old rules. There are descendants of Lord Chunli's brother, Lord Maoxi (name Sheng), currently living in Weidu, Jingxi, whose genealogy matches ours. Due to the great distance in time and the inability to verify details, we should each adhere to the principle of "hearsay differs" to show our adherence to tradition.

Article 9:
Our clan's genealogy was first compiled in the tenth year of Shunzhi, long after the initial migration. Since the revision in the Xinhai year of Qianlong, another hundred years have passed, and now another fifty years have gone by. Many records have been lost or cannot be verified. During the last revision, branches from places like Minhuang and Dashuji Village came to the bureau for revision, but their connection to the old genealogy was unclear, so a supplementary volume was made to detail their branches for future verification. Now, branches from Benniu Town and other places also have no connectable lineage, so their generations are recorded in the supplementary genealogy, following the principle of leaving doubts unresolved. Those supplementary records from the old genealogy that were not submitted this time are also briefly appended at the end.

Article 10:
This revision of the genealogy follows the old regulations, with each branch donating funds for compilation. Any shortfall is covered by the generous donations of clan members serving as officials or secretaries abroad, and a separate record of donations is printed and distributed with the genealogy.

Article 11:
This time, the old genealogies are numbered and distributed to specific individuals for safekeeping. The number and branch are clearly listed at the end of the book. A total of one hundred copies have been printed for future revisions and inspections.
