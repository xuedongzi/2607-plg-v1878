# 毘陵呂氏增修族譜 序

---

### 毘陵呂氏增修族譜序 (1878)

```text
宗法廢而譜學興敬宗收族之
意猶少有存焉者自人心不古
并視此為具文於是聽其漶漫
而不修日久愈畏其難而愈不
能修彼固未嘗為一本計漸至
視如塗人而不恤也吾呂氏自

明永樂間由宜興遷郡五傳而
分三大分支孫散處鄉城日益
蕃昌世為望族宗譜肇自順治
癸巳始修於乾隆辛亥再修於
道光庚子其間多有闕而不具
者以前此曠而不修且百有餘
載也矧經咸豐庚申粵逆之亂

當時流亡相繼糾恤無從同治
甲子官軍收復常城江南底定
宗人稍稍安集復我邦族而料
檢丁口半為國殤外此則遷徙
流離存亡莫必尚及今不修大
懼怕亂先典羼讹来乘不第有
闕而不具之憾歲辛未金誠懇

午創議纂輯懋勲懋榮倡捐經
費商之嗣彬始有端緒癸酉贄
庭族兄自山左歸老慨然身任
次第興修越歲兄賫志以歿其
事復寢丁丑繼午春闈罷歸力
踵前議志在必行當祭掃石塔
始塋楫族人而告之詢謀僉同

於是綜核名實分別纂換喪亂
以來族情不一紓者糾之漏者
補之訛者訂之不衰於義者正
之不詳於例者益之崇祀典而
節義彰定祭法而昭穆序重嫡
派而統序不能紊嚴名分而嫡
庶不敢淆凡一節之媺一行之

善靡不廣為搜采闡發幽芳旁
及塋地田畝勾稽詳審條分縷
晰如指上螺為圖四為表十為
志六為傳八遵舊制而加廣焉
計此舉經始於辛未之秋告成
於戊寅之冬中間時作時輟遷
延八載卒能遠紹旁搜越十有

三月而蕆事外此與其役者十
五世嗣彬則稽覈丁數十六世
金誠則綜司各職勤而有功懋
蕃家籤則校讐參酌與十七世
德驥等均有力焉書成繼午授
予曰斯譜之作中經寇亂舛譌
實多惟不逞智以紊舊章不循

情以乖禮法而已予受而讀之
嘉其始終勤勞不因陋就簡聊
以塞責既悲贄庭兄之不及見
而又幸斯譜之卒底於有成也
因慨然曰我呂氏自明遷常四
百餘年於茲矣回溯積學敦行
代有隱德至

聖清而大顯簪纓勿替軒服重
輝即伏處田間而食德者說禮
敦詩服疇者橫經負耒各能砥
勵廉隅勉勉循循無自遐棄後
之讀是譜者庶幾知所觀感油
然生其孝弟之心用是經明行
修蔚為國華永足光前而裕後

不僅有敬宗收族之效也老夫
灌園猥以輩行較尊序其事以
為族人勸
光緒四年歲次戊寅仲冬十三
世孫洪盛謹序
```

### 现代汉语 简体字

宗法废而谱学兴，敬宗收族的用意还稍有留存。自从人心不古，大家把这当成流于形式的空文，于是任由它模糊散佚而不修订，时间越久越畏惧难度，就越发没法完成续修。族人从来没有从同根同源的角度考量，渐渐把同族之人视作路人，毫无体恤之心。

我吕氏家族从明永乐年间由宜兴迁居常州，传五代后分出三大支脉，子孙散居城乡，人口日益繁盛，世代都是当地望族。族谱最早在顺治癸巳年启动编修，乾隆辛亥年首次修成，道光庚子年完成第二次续修，期间内容多有缺漏不全，因为此前荒废不修的时长已经超过百年。又恰逢咸丰庚申年的战乱，当时族人接连流亡，连聚拢抚恤都无从谈起。同治甲子年官军收复常州城，江南局势平定，族人才陆续返乡安顿，重建宗族秩序。清点人口时，半数族人在战乱中罹难，其余的人迁徙流离，生死下落不明。如果当下还不启动修谱，很怕旧有的宗族典制混乱，错漏内容混入其中，不只是内容缺漏不全的遗憾。
同治十年辛未，吕金诚（字恳午）率先倡议纂修族谱，吕懋勋、吕懋荣带头捐助经费，和吕嗣彬商议后，修谱才初步有了头绪。同治十二年癸酉，族兄吕贽庭从山东辞官归乡，慨然主动承担主修职责，一步步推进修谱事务。没想到次年族兄抱憾离世，修谱的事又被迫搁置。光绪三年丁丑，吕继午参加春闱落第返乡，全力接续之前的修谱计划，下定决心一定要完成。在祭扫石塔始迁祖的墓地时，他召集族人告知此事，所有人都一致赞同。

之后大家核查名实，分类整理修订：战乱以来宗族事务有疏漏的地方就补齐，有错讹的内容就订正，不符合道义的内容就修正，旧例里没写清楚的部分就补充完善。尊崇祭祀典制让节义事迹得到彰显，定立祭祀规则让长幼次序分明，重视嫡传支脉让宗族统序不会紊乱，严格名分边界让嫡庶秩序不会混淆。但凡有一处善行、一项美德，都广泛搜集记录，把隐没的善举发掘出来。连祖墓、族田的信息都核查得十分细致，条目清晰分明，像数自己手指上的纹路一样清楚。全谱设置四幅图、十张表、六类志、八篇传，在旧有体例基础上做了扩充。这次修谱从辛未年秋天启动，到戊寅年冬天完成，中间断断续续推进，拖延了八年，最终广泛搜集远亲近支的信息，耗时十三个月全部收尾。

参与这项事务的族人还有：十五世孙吕嗣彬负责核查人口数量，十六世孙吕金诚统筹各项事务，勤恳出力；吕懋蕃、吕家签负责校对斟酌，和十七世孙吕德骥等人都付出了不少心力。谱稿完成后，吕继午把文稿交给我，说这次修谱历经战乱，错漏内容很多，只求不凭小聪明打乱旧有的宗族规制，不徇私情违背礼法准则就好。

我读完这份文稿，赞许他们全程勤恳踏实，没有敷衍了事、因陋就简应付交差，既为吕贽庭兄没能亲眼看到谱成而惋惜，又为这份族谱最终顺利完成而庆幸。我不由得感慨：我吕氏从明代迁居常州至今已有四百多年，回望家族世代积累学识、敦厚品行，代代都有不为人知的善德，到清代家族声望达到鼎盛，世代为官、荣耀不断。就连隐居乡野的族人，也都通晓礼义、熟读诗书；耕种田地的族人，也能一边耕作一边研读经典，人人都能砥砺品行，勤勉守礼，不自暴自弃。后世读到这份族谱的人，或许能有所触动，自然而然生出孝悌之心，进而做到明经修行，成为国家的栋梁之才，完全可以光前裕后，不只是实现敬宗收族的作用而已。我隐居田园，只是凭着在族中辈分稍高，记录下这件事，用来勉励全族后人。

光绪四年岁次戊寅仲冬，十三世孙吕洪盛谨序。

### Preface to the Revised Genealogy of the Lü Clan in Piling (1878)

Since feudal patriarchal systems declined, genealogy studies have thrived, preserving the last remnants of the spirit of "honoring ancestors and uniting the clan". As social morals deteriorated, people began to treat genealogies as empty formalities, allowing records to fall into disrepair. Over time, the task grew so daunting that no one dared to resume it, as no one considered the shared roots of the clan, gradually treating fellow clansmen as strangers without compassion.

Our Lü clan moved from Yixing to Changzhou during the Yongle era of the Ming Dynasty. After five generations, it split into three major branches, whose descendants spread across urban and rural areas, growing into a prominent local clan. The earliest genealogy of our clan was initiated in the Guisi year of the Shunzhi reign, first completed in the Xinhai year of the Qianlong reign, and revised for the second time in the Gengzi year of the Daoguang reign. Large gaps remained in the records, as over a hundred years had passed without updates. Worse still, the Gengshen year war during the Xianfeng reign displaced countless clansmen, making it impossible to gather and support each other. In the Jiazi year of the Tongzhi reign, government forces recaptured Changzhou, and peace was restored in southern China. Surviving clansmen gradually returned to rebuild the clan, but half of the recorded population had died in the war, while the rest were displaced, their fates unknown. If we delayed the revision any longer, we feared the ancient clan records would be irreparably corrupted, with far more gaps than just missing entries.

In the Xinwei year (1871), Lü Jincheng (styled Kenwu) first proposed compiling the revised genealogy, with Lü Maoxun and Lü Maorong donating funds. With the support of Lü Sibin, the project finally got off the ground. In the Guiyou year (1873), my clansman Lü Zhiting returned home from his official post in Shandong, volunteering to lead the revision. Unfortunately, he passed away the following year with his ambition unfulfilled, and the project was suspended. In the Dingchou year (1877), Lü Jiwu returned home after failing the imperial spring examination, and resolved to carry on the unfinished work. When he led the clansmen to sweep the tomb of the first ancestor in Shita, everyone unanimously agreed to restart the project.

The team verified all names and facts, sorting and revising records: they corrected omissions, fixed errors, revised content that violated moral principles, and supplemented details missing in old conventions. They honored sacrificial rituals to promote loyalty and righteousness, standardized ancestral ceremonies to clarify generational order, prioritized direct lineages to avoid disrupting the clan's lineage, and strictly defined status hierarchies to maintain proper order between legitimate and concubinal descendants. Every virtuous act and good deed was widely collected to highlight unrecognized kindness. Even the locations and acreage of ancestral graves and clan fields were carefully verified, organized with perfect clarity. The final genealogy includes 4 illustrations, 10 tables, 6 categories of records, and 8 biographies, expanding on the old system. The project started in autumn 1871 and was completed in winter 1878. After eight years of intermittent work, the team collected records of distant and close relatives, finishing the full revision in 13 months.

Other contributors include: 15th-generation descendant Lü Sibin, who verified population records; 16th-generation descendant Lü Jincheng, who managed all daily operations with great diligence; Lü Maofan and Lü Jiaqian, who proofread and reviewed the text, alongside 17th-generation descendant Lü Deji and others who made key contributions. When the manuscript was finished, Lü Jiwu handed it to me, explaining that this genealogy had gone through wars with countless errors, and their principle was never to tamper with old clan traditions out of cleverness, nor to violate rituals and laws for personal favors.

After reading the manuscript, I praised their consistent diligence and refusal to cut corners. I grieved that Lü Zhiting never lived to see the finished work, but rejoiced that the genealogy was finally completed. I can't help but reflect: our Lü clan has lived in Changzhou for over 400 years since the Ming Dynasty. For generations, we have valued scholarship and moral conduct, accumulating hidden virtues that brought great prosperity to the clan in the Qing Dynasty, with generations of officials bringing lasting honor. Even clansmen living in rural villages value rituals and poetry, and farmers study classics while plowing the fields. Everyone strives to uphold integrity, behaving prudently and never abandoning themselves. Future generations reading this genealogy will surely be inspired to naturally develop filial piety and fraternal love, cultivate moral character through scholarship, and become outstanding talents serving the country, bringing glory to ancestors and benefiting descendants. This goes far beyond the simple goal of "honoring ancestors and uniting the clan". As a retired elder who tends to my garden, I write this preface only because of my seniority in the clan, to encourage all descendants.

Written in the mid-winter of the Wuyin year, the 4th year of the Guangxu reign (1878), by Hong Sheng, 13th-generation descendant of the Lü clan.

### Chinese-English Reference Notes

1. ‌Zongpu (Genealogy, 宗谱)‌
    - 背景注释：是中国宗族文化中记录家族世系、人物事迹、族规家训的核心典籍，毗陵吕氏宗谱为清光绪四年木活字本，共二十二卷，是常州吕氏家族传承的核心文献。
2. ‌Jingzong Shouzu (Honoring Ancestors and Uniting the Clan, 敬宗收族)‌
    - 背景注释：传统宗族的核心宗旨，通过修谱、祭祖等活动强化族人对共同祖先的认同，凝聚全族向心力，是清代江南望族维系宗族秩序的核心理念。
3. ‌Yongle Era of Ming Dynasty (明永乐年间)‌
    - 背景注释：对应公元1403-1424年，是毗陵吕氏始迁祖吕成从宜兴迁居常州的时间，家族自此在常州落地生根，逐步发展为当地望族。
4. ‌Shunzhi Guisi (顺治癸巳)‌
    - 背景注释：对应公元1653年，是吕氏家族首次启动宗谱编修的起始年份，为家族最早的修谱记录。
5. ‌Qianlong Xinhai (乾隆辛亥)‌
    - 背景注释：对应公元1791年，是吕氏宗谱首次正式修成的年份，填补了此前家族世系记录的空白。
6. ‌Daoguang Gengzi (道光庚子)‌
    - 背景注释：对应公元1840年，是吕氏第二次续修族谱的年份，该版本为后续光绪年间的增修提供了核心底本。
7. ‌Xianfeng Gengshen (咸丰庚申)‌
    - 背景注释：对应公元1860年，是太平军攻破常州的战乱年份，吕氏家族大量族人罹难、流离，族谱资料散佚，给后续修谱造成极大阻碍。
8. ‌Tongzhi Jiazi (同治甲子)‌
    - 背景注释：对应公元1864年，清军收复常州，江南局势逐步平定，吕氏族人开始返乡重建宗族秩序。
9. ‌Lü Jincheng (吕金诚, 书猷公，styled Kenwu)‌
    - 背景注释：十六世吕氏族人，是本次光绪增修族谱的核心发起者之一，统筹修谱各项日常事务，现存光绪版《毗陵吕氏族谱》明确标注其为主要纂修人。
    - ‌Lü Jincheng (吕金诚) was the architect and owner of Xuedong Lane - ‌Lü Residence (雪洞巷吕宅)。
    - ‌Lü Jincheng (吕金诚) is ‌Lü Fan's 2nd great-grandfather, or in Chinese "玄祖父".
10. ‌Lü Zhiting (吕贽庭)‌
    - 背景注释：吕氏十三世族人，曾在山东任职，归乡后主动承担修谱主修职责，后不幸早逝，未能亲眼见到谱成，是家族修谱史上的重要人物。
11. ‌Lü Jiwu (吕继午)‌
    - 背景注释：吕氏族人，在吕贽庭离世后接续修谱事业，在春闱落第返乡后全力推进修谱工作，最终完成这部耗时八年的增修族谱。
12. ‌Zhaomu (Generational Order, 昭穆)‌
    - 背景注释：传统宗族的核心礼制，规定宗庙、墓地、世系中长幼尊卑的排列次序，明确代际辈分，避免宗族世系出现混乱。
13. ‌Guangxu Wuyin (光绪四年戊寅)‌
    - 背景注释：对应公元1878年，是本次《毗陵吕氏增修族谱》最终完稿、作序的年份，该版本为木活字本，共二十二卷，完整留存了清代常州吕氏的家族信息。
