# 吕适 - 成13

## 吕适 （参三公）- 成13

### 世表条目

吕适 

* 雪洞巷吕家祠神像第一位
* 在家族同辈中排行第三
* 字参三。身份为太学士。
* 因为孙子吕志恒的功绩，被朝廷追赠（貤赠）为奉直大夫、盐运司运副。
    - 后来，又依照补任福建噶玛兰通判的旧例，被晋封为朝议大夫、福建台湾府知府。
* 生卒
    - 他生于康熙壬寅年十一月二十八日丑时（1722-01-15 凌晨1点-3点）
    - 卒于乾隆乙巳年正月二十八日卯时（1785-03-09 早晨5点-7点）
    - 享年六十四岁
* 他的具体生平事迹，详见其个人小传（见后）
* 他的正妻是高氏
    - 被朝廷追赠为“宜人”，依照例制晋封为“恭人”。
    - 高氏出身显赫，是正红旗副都统高鼎的曾孙女、高世荣的孙女、太学生兼候选州同知高文孝的第四个女儿。
    - 高氏生于康熙辛丑年（1721年）二月十六日戌时（晚上7点-9点），卒于嘉庆丙辰年（1796年）二月初三日申时（下午3点-5点），享年七十六岁。
* 夫妇二人合葬于三宝桥的新墓地（新阡）主穴，风水坐向为“壬山丙向”（即坐正北偏东，向正南偏西）。
* 他们育有二子二女：
    - 长子为吕景谦
    - 次子为吕景正。
    - 长女嫁给了太学生高重光（他是高文孝的孙子、附贡生高琎的长子）。
    - 次女嫁给了丁葆光（他是乾隆丙辰年举人丁赞虞的孙子、本县增生丁书升的儿子）。


**族谱世表原文**：

适，行三，字参三。太学士。 貤赠奉直大夫，盐运司运副。借补福建噶玛兰通判例，晋朝议大夫，福建台湾府知府。生康熙壬寅十一月二十八日丑时，卒乾隆乙巳正月二十八日卯时，寿六十四。事见小传。配高氏。貤赠宜人，例晋恭人。正红旗副都统讳鼎曾孙女，讳世荣孙女，太学生候选州同知讳文孝四女。生康熙辛丑二月十六日戊时，卒嘉庆丙辰二月初三日申时，寿七十六岁。合葬三宝桥新阡主穴壬山丙向。子二：长景谦，次景正。女二，长适太学生高重光，即文孝孙附贡生讳琎长子。次适丁葆光，乾隆丙辰举人讳赞虞孙，邑增生讳书升子。

#### 解析

1. **生卒年公历换算**：
   * **吕适**：生于 **1722年1月15日**，卒于 **1785年3月9日**。
   * **高氏**：生于 **1721年3月13日**，卒于 **1796年3月11日**。
   * *注：高氏比吕适大将近一岁，且比吕适多活了11年。*

2. **官职与荣耀（貤赠与例晋）**：
   * 吕适本人是“太学士”，但他获得了“朝议大夫、福建台湾府知府”这样极高的正五品官衔。这在清代通常是因为他的后代（如吕志恒）在朝廷做官，皇帝将荣誉“貤赠”给先祖。这证明了雪洞巷吕氏在清代中期的鼎盛地位。

3. **联姻（高氏背景）**：
   * 吕适的妻子高氏出身于**正红旗**（清代八旗之一），其曾祖父高鼎官至“副都统”（正二品武官）。这反映了当时常州地方望族与八旗权贵之间存在着紧密的社会与政治联姻网络。
    - 八旗每旗又分：满，汉，蒙

4. **儿女亲家**：
   * 两个女儿分别嫁给了高家和丁家，且男方都是“太学生”、“举人”或“增生”（优秀的生员）。这种“亲上加亲”和“士绅联姻”，是古代家族巩固地方势力的重要手段。

5. **风水与祖茔**：
   * “合葬三宝桥新阡主穴壬山丙向”：这为您日后寻访常州祖茔提供了极其精确的地理和风水坐标（三宝桥、坐北朝南）。

6. 根据清代命妇体系，具体的封号等级列表如下：
    * 一品、二品：称“夫人”
    * 三品：称“淑人”
    * 四品：称“恭人”
    * 五品：称“宜人”
    * 六品：称“安人”
    * 七品及以下：称“孺人”

#### 现代汉语 简体字 标点

参三公性格豪爽，喜欢畅谈古今的事情。他写文章才思非常敏捷，大家都用“倚马之才”（形容文思敏捷，能立刻写出好文章）来称赞他。

然而，他多次参加科举考试都未能考中。丁卯年时，他洋洋洒洒地写了一篇关于五经的长文章，本以为这次一定能金榜题名，结果还是落榜了。己卯年，他又去京城赶考，最终依然失意而归。

从那以后，他便在家乡悠闲地生活，再也不把科举考试放在心上了。他每天和亲朋好友吟诗饮酒、互相往来；他为人轻财仗义，如果能做一两件好事，就会觉得非常痛快。

他在一首抒发情怀的诗里写道：“把落榜的考卷统统烧掉，不管走到哪里，只管痛饮美酒。”后来，他因为过度纵情（饮酒）而生病，去世时享年六十四岁。

### Genealogical Record

**Lü Shi**

* The first ancestral portrait enshrined in the Lü Family Shrine at Xuedong Alley.
* Ranked third among his male siblings.
* Courtesy name: Sansan (参三). Held the title of Imperial Academy Student (Taixuesheng).
* Posthumously honored by the imperial court as Fengzhi Dafu (Gentleman of Meritorious Service) and Assistant Salt Transport Commissioner, in recognition of the achievements of his grandson, Lü Zhiheng.
    - Later, under the precedent of his appointment as the Sub-prefect of Kavalan (Gamaran), Fujian, he was further elevated to Chaoyi Dafu (Grand Master for Court Consultation) and Prefect of Taiwan Prefecture, Fujian.
* **Birth and Death**
    - Born on the 28th day of the 11th lunar month in the Renyin year of the Kangxi reign, during the Chou hour (1:00 AM – 3:00 AM), which corresponds to January 15, 1722.
    - Passed away on the 28th day of the 1st lunar month in the Yisi year of the Qianlong reign, during the Mao hour (5:00 AM – 7:00 AM), which corresponds to March 9, 1785.
    - Lived to the age of 64.
* Details of his life and deeds are documented in his personal biography (see below).
* **Wife:** Lady Gao
    - Posthumously honored as Yiren (Lady of Grace), and later elevated to Gongren (Lady of Reverence) according to imperial decrees.
    - Lady Gao came from a prominent family. She was the great-granddaughter of Gao Ding, the Vice Commander of the Bordered Red Banner; the granddaughter of Gao Shirong; and the fourth daughter of Gao Wenxiao, an Imperial Academy Student and candidate for Sub-prefectural Magistrate.
    - Born on the 16th day of the 2nd lunar month in the Xinchou year of the Kangxi reign, during the Xu hour (7:00 PM – 9:00 PM), corresponding to 1721.
    - Passed away on the 3rd day of the 2nd lunar month in the Bingchen year of the Jiaqing reign, during the Shen hour (3:00 PM – 5:00 PM), corresponding to 1796.
    - Lived to the age of 76.
* The couple was buried together in the main tomb of the newly established cemetery (Xinqian) at Sanbao Bridge, with the feng shui orientation of "Ren Shan Bing Xiang" (sitting slightly east of due north, facing slightly west of due south).
* **Children:** Two sons and two daughters.
    - Eldest son: Lü Jingqian.
    - Second son: Lü Jingzheng.
    - Eldest daughter: Married Gao Chongguang, an Imperial Academy Student (grandson of Gao Wenxiao and eldest son of Gao Jin, a Supplementary Tribute Student).
    - Second daughter: Married Ding Baoguang (grandson of Ding Zanyu, a provincial graduate (Juren) in the Bingchen year of the Qianlong reign, and son of Ding Shusheng, a local Supplementary Licentiate).

#### Biography

Lord Sansan had a bold and open personality, and he loved to discuss history and current affairs at length. He possessed an exceptionally quick and brilliant mind for writing; people often praised him for having the "talent of leaning against a horse" (an idiom describing a literary genius capable of composing excellent essays on the spot).

However, he repeatedly failed the imperial civil service examinations. In the Dingmao year, he wrote a sweeping, comprehensive essay on the Five Classics, fully confident that he would finally pass, but he was once again rejected. In the Jimao year, he traveled to the capital to take the exams again, only to return home in disappointment.

After that, he settled into a leisurely life in his hometown and no longer concerned himself with the pursuit of official degrees. He spent his days composing poetry, drinking wine, and socializing with relatives and friends. He was generous with his wealth and fiercely loyal to his friends, finding immense joy and satisfaction whenever he could do a good deed for others.

In a poem expressing his innermost feelings, he wrote: "Burn all the failed examination papers; wherever I go, I shall only drink my fill." Later in life, he fell ill due to his excessive indulgence in wine, and he passed away at the age of 64.
