# 吕泽均 - 成18

## 吕泽均 - 成18

### 世表

吕泽均

* 排行第一（长子）
* 字少銓，号卿官。
* 生辰
    - 生于光绪甲午年十二月初三日申时 下午3点-5点 （1894-12-29）

* 订婚镇江丹徒县的朱氏为妻
    - 朱氏出身显赫，其家族获得了四品封典。
    - 朱氏是上元县教谕朱伟景祺的孙女、拥有同知衔的分省知县朱沂（字长水）的长女。
    - 朱氏生于光绪壬辰年九月十八日辰时 早晨7点-9点（1892-11-07）

* 世表原文

泽均，行一，字少銓。号卿官。生光绪甲午十二月初三日申时。聘镇江丹徒县朱氏四品封典上元县教讳伟景祺孙女，同知衔分省知县印沂长水。生光绪壬辰九月十八日辰时。

### The genealogical record

**Lü Zejun**

* **Birth Order:** Firstborn son
* **Courtesy Name (Zi):** Shaoquan (少銓)
* **Pseudonym (Hao):** Qingguan (卿官)
* **Birth:**
    * Born on the 3rd day of the 12th lunar month in the Jiawu year of the Guangxu reign (1894), during the Shen hour (3:00 PM – 5:00 PM), corresponding to December 29, 1894.
* **Engagement:** Engaged to Lady Zhu from Dantu County, Zhenjiang.
    * Lady Zhu came from a prominent family that had been awarded a fourth-rank posthumous title.
    * She was the granddaughter of Zhu Weijingqi, the Educational Instructor (Jiaoyu) of Shangyuan County, and the eldest daughter of Zhu Yi (courtesy name Changshui), a Sub-prefectural Magistrate assigned to a province with the title of Assistant Prefect (Tongzhi).
    * Born on the 18th day of the 9th lunar month in the Renchen year of the Guangxu reign (1892), during the Chen hour (7:00 AM – 9:00 AM), corresponding to November 7, 1892.
