# 吕景正 - 成14

## 吕景正 （养园） - 成14

### 世表条目

吕景正

* 雪洞巷吕家祠神像第二位
* 在家中排行第二
* 字祠穆，号养园。
* 他是候选县佐，后来因为孙子吕金诚获得候选通判的身份，被朝廷额外钦加同知衔、再叠加一级官阶，按照清代的貤赠制度，被追赠为朝议大夫。
* 生卒
    - 生于乾隆二十年五月初六子时 （1755-06-15）
    - 卒于道光五年九月二十八日戌时 （1829-10-25）
    - 享年七十五岁
* 相关生平事迹另有单独的小传记载
* 他的原配夫人是徐氏，徐氏是
    - 太学生、候选县佐徐振九的长女
    - 凭借孙子吕金诚的官阶，被貤赠为恭人
    - 徐氏生于乾隆十八年二月二十二日戌时 （1753-03-26）
    - 卒于嘉庆二十五年十一月十五子时 （1820-12-20）
    - 享年六十八岁
* 去世后吕景正和徐氏合葬在父亲坟茔东侧的主墓穴中
* 两人共育有三个儿子和三个女儿：
    - 长子吕籍馥
    - 次子吕兰
    - 三子吕滋福
    - 长女嫁给了浙江杭州府江涨务大使黄朝阳的儿子黄观国
    - 次女嫁给了太学生高迎旭的儿子、候选县佐高金诏
    - 三女嫁给了太学生汤辰启的儿子汤征吉
* 世表原文

景正，行二，字祠穆，号养园。候选县佐，以孙金诚候选通判。钦加同知衔加一级。貤赠朝议大夫。生乾隆乙亥五月初六日子时，卒道光乙丑九月二十八日戌时，寿七十五。事件小传。配徐氏。貤赠恭人。太学生、候选县佐讳振九长女，生乾隆葵酉二月二十二日戌时，卒嘉庆庚辰十一月十五日十子时，寿六十八。合葬父茔东偏主穴。子三：长籍馥，次兰，三滋福。女三：长适浙江杭州府江涨务大使黄观国讳朝阳子。次适候选县佐高金诏，太学生讳迎旭子。三适汤征吉，太学生讳辰启子。

### Genealogical Record

Lü Jingzheng‌

* Rank among siblings: The second son
* Courtesy name: Cimu
* Art name: Yangyuan
* Official title: Candidate District Assistant (houxuan xianzuo). Due to his grandson Lü Jincheng being a Candidate Assistant Prefect (houxuan tongpan), he was graciously granted the rank of Sub-Prefect (tongzhi) with one additional grade promotion by imperial decree, and posthumously conferred the title of Grand Master of Court Discussion (chaoyi dafu) through the yi-zeng (posthumous honor extended to ancestors by descendant's official rank) system.
Birth: The midnight (zi shi) of the 6th day of the 5th lunar month, the 20th year of Qianlong Reign (1755)
Death: The dusk (xu shi) of the 28th day of the 9th lunar month, the 5th year of Daoguang Reign (1825)
Lifespan: 75 years old. His detailed biography is recorded in a separate short account.

* Spouse: Lady Xu‌
    - Posthumous title: Gongren (fourth-rank official matron in Qing dynasty honor system), conferred through the yi-zeng system
    - Family background: The eldest daughter of Xu Zhenjiu, a Imperial Academy Student (taixuesheng) and Candidate District Assistant
    - Birth: The dusk (xu shi) of the 22nd day of the 2nd lunar month, the 18th year of Qianlong Reign (1753)
    - Death: The midnight (zi shi) of the 15th day of the 11th lunar month, the 25th year of Jiaqing Reign (1820)
    - Lifespan: 68 years old. 
    - She was buried jointly with Lü Jingzheng in the main tomb located to the east of his father's graveyard.

* Descendants‌
    - Sons (3 in total): 
        * The eldest son Lü Jifu, 
        * the second son Lü Lan, 
        * the third son Lü Zifu
    - Daughters (3 in total):
        * The eldest daughter married Huang Guanguo, 
            - Son of Huang Chaoyang, who served as the Jiangzhangwu Taxation Station Magistrate in Hangzhou Prefecture, Zhejiang Province
        * The second daughter married Gao Jinzhao, a Candidate District Assistant 
            - Son of Gao Yingxu, who was an Imperial Academy Student
        * The third daughter married Tang Zhengji, 
            - Son of Tang Chenqi, who was an Imperial Academy Student
